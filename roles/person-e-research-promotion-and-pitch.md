# Person E: Research, testing, promotion and pitch

Read [shared.md](shared.md) first: it has the goal, ground rules, folder ownership, the `lib/db.ts` data functions, the timeline and the demo script.

Owns the half of the judging that isn't code: real student need, feedback and improvements, promotion and the pitch.

1. **Validate the problem (start tonight):**
   - Build a Google Form survey of 8 questions or fewer, for example:
     - Have you lost something on campus this year?
     - Did you get it back?
     - How did you look for it?
     - Have you hit a construction zone or broken elevator that forced a detour?
     - How do you report campus problems today?
     - Would points toward a meal voucher make you return found items?
   - Post it to SJSU Discord servers, GroupMe chats, r/SJSU and friends, aiming for 30+ responses by 9 AM.
   - Do 5 short interviews tonight or tomorrow morning. If someone with a mobility or vision need is willing to talk, ask what they'd want from obstacle alerts, but never pressure anyone.
2. **Problem definition:** write the problem statement and 2–3 personas in Momentum AI Studio, backed by survey numbers and quotes.
3. **Seed content:** give Person B 25 realistic SJSU report titles and locations by Friday 9 PM (for example "Bottle filler red light, Student Union 2nd floor").
4. **Usability test (Sat 9:30–10:30)** with 3–5 students on the deployed build:
   - Tasks: report a lost item, find the obstacle ahead and its detour on the 3D map, redeem a voucher.
   - Time each task, note where people hesitate, and ask for a 1–5 ease rating.
   - Hand the top 3 fixes to Person A right away, and capture before/after screenshots for a "What we changed after feedback" slide.
5. **Promotion plan:**
   - **Who:** first-wave users are freshmen in campus housing, students who lose things often, and accessibility users reached through the Accessible Education Center.
   - **Channels:** orientation, Associated Students, RA floor meetings, Instagram and TikTok "we found it" stories, and QR stickers on water fillers and the Lost & Found desk.
   - **Partners:** Associated Students or campus dining for vouchers, Facilities for fixes.
   - **Measuring success:** use the success metrics from MASTER.md.
6. **Pitch:**
   - About 8 slides: problem and evidence, who it's for, the solution, live demo, feedback and what we changed, promotion, ethics and safety (privacy, accessibility, no hazardous missions), and the ask.
   - Write the demo script with Person A, and record a backup demo video at 11:30 on Saturday.
   - Prepare Q&A answers on privacy, abuse, funding and why the map is 3D.
7. **Process check:** make sure every Momentum AI Studio stage is filled in: problem definition, design, prototype, feedback, promotion and pitch.

**Done when:** the slides, backup video, survey results, test notes and promotion plan are ready, and every Momentum AI Studio stage is complete.
